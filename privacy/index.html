Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

Who I am

Prompted is a free educational YouTube channel (@PromptedChannel) and this website, run by the channel's creator ("I"). For the GDPR, I am the data controller. To reach me, reply to any Prompted email or message @PromptedChannel on YouTube.

What I collect

When you ask for a free resource: your email address, your first name if you give it, whether you ticked the box to get new guides, your browser's time zone (only to show the right default for that box) and the time you signed up. I don't collect anything else on purpose.

Why, and on what basis

  • To give you the free resource you asked for. It's shown on the page right away; no email is needed for that.
  • If you ticked the box: to email you new guides and updates, about once a week at most. In the EU, the UK and Canada this is based on your consent, and the box starts unticked there. You can withdraw consent at any time.

If you didn't tick the box, I don't add you to my mailing list and don't send you emails. Your request is kept only as a record of the download.

Who processes it

  • Kit (kit.com) stores the email of people who ticked the box and sends my emails, as my processor.
  • A small server I run keeps a backup copy of each request, so nothing is lost if Kit is down.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may log IP addresses. The page loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address.

I don't sell your data and don't share it for advertising.

How long I keep it

Mailing list: until you unsubscribe; after that you stop getting emails right away and I keep only the record that you unsubscribed. Download records: until you ask me to delete them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California), you can ask me to show, correct or delete your data and to stop emailing you. Reply to any Prompted email or message @PromptedChannel on YouTube. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Unsubscribing

Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. Requests are processed right away and always within 10 business days.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child gave me their email, tell me and I'll delete it.

Cookies and tracking

This site uses no cookies and no analytics. I don't track you across other sites; Do Not Track signals don't change anything here because there is no tracking to turn off.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the effective date.
