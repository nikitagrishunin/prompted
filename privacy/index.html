Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

Who I am

Prompted is a free educational YouTube channel (@PromptedChannel) and this website, run by the channel's creator ("I"). For the GDPR, I am the data controller. To reach me, reply to any Prompted email or message @PromptedChannel on YouTube.

What I collect

When you use the form to get a free resource: your email address (the form needs it), your first name if you give it, whether you ticked the box to get new guides, the version of the box text you saw, your browser's time zone (used to decide whether the box starts ticked, and kept as a record of what you saw) and the date and time of the request. I don't collect anything else on purpose. You are not required by law or contract to give me this data.

Why, and on what basis

  • Sending you new guides and updates (about once a week at most) - only if you ticked the box. Basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); express consent in Canada). You can withdraw it at any time, as easily as you gave it, with the unsubscribe link in every email. Withdrawing doesn't affect anything done before.
  • Keeping a record of your request and what you agreed to (email, name, box choice, box text version, time zone, time), so I can prove consent, handle unsubscribe and deletion requests and stop abuse of the form. Basis: my legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). You can object to this at any time.

If you didn't tick the box, I don't add you to my mailing list, don't email you and don't use your data for anything else. I make no automated decisions about you and do no profiling.

Who receives it, and where it goes

  • Kit (ConvertKit, LLC, United States) receives the email and first name of people who ticked the box, stores them and sends my emails, as my processor.
  • A server I rent stores every request (email, name, box choice, box text version, time zone, time) - with or without the box ticked.
  • My own computer and an encrypted backup on Google Drive (Google, United States) hold copies of those records.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, United States) hosts this website and may log IP addresses. The page loads fonts from Google Fonts (Google, United States), which receives your IP address.

I'm based outside the EU, the UK and the United States, so your data crosses borders, including to the United States. I don't sell your data and don't share it for advertising.

How long I keep it

Mailing list (Kit): until you unsubscribe; after that I keep your email and the fact that you unsubscribed, so I don't email you again. Records of requests (with or without the box ticked): until you ask me to delete them. If you ask, I delete your data within one month, including from my backups as they are replaced.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California), you can ask me to show you your data, correct it, delete it, restrict its use, give it to you in a portable form, or stop using it (object), and you can withdraw consent at any time. Reply to any Prompted email or message @PromptedChannel on YouTube; I answer within one month. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Unsubscribing

Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. Unsubscribe requests are processed as soon as possible and always within 10 business days.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 16, and I don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child gave me their email, tell me and I'll delete it.

Cookies and tracking

This site uses no cookies and no analytics. I don't track you across other sites, and I don't let advertising or analytics companies collect data about you on this site. The hosting and font providers named above can see your IP address when the page loads. Do Not Track signals don't change anything here because there is no tracking to turn off.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and its effective date. If the change is material, I'll also tell the people on my mailing list by email.
